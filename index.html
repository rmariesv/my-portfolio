About

I am a third-year BS Computer Engineering student at National University – Fairview, passionate about exploring the intersection of creativity and technology.

With interests in machine learning, hardware engineering, software development, and graphic design, I passionately enjoy combining technical problem-solving with creative thinking to develop functional and visually appealing solutions.

Throughout my academic journey, I have gained experience in both hardware and software development while exploring different aspects of engineering, from circuit design and application development to UI/UX design and visual communication. I enjoy taking part in both the technical and creative sides of projects, whether developing functional systems, designing user interfaces, or creating posters and presentations. I believe that a balance between functionality and thoughtful design can make technology more accessible and meaningful.

Outside of academics, I enjoy expressing myself through writing and drawing.

I am always eager to learn, experiment with emerging technologies, and expand my skills as both an engineer and a designer, with the goal of creating solutions that combine innovation, usability, and creativity.
